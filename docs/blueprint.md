# **App Name**: CarbonChain

## Core Features:

- Carbon Credit Issuance: Issue new carbon credits on the blockchain upon successful verification, recording the project details and methodology used for quantification.
- IoT Device Integration: Integrate data feeds from IoT devices deployed at carbon offset projects (e.g., forestry, renewable energy) to continuously monitor and verify carbon sequestration or emissions reduction.
- Automated Verification Tool: Employ AI to continuously audit IoT data and on-site audit data to compare the reported figures, detecting anomalies and potentially fraudulent claims, using external information as a tool for verification
- Carbon Credit Tracking: Enable transparent tracking of carbon credits across their lifecycle, from issuance to retirement, recording all transactions on the blockchain.
- Proof-of-Impact Reporting: Generate detailed reports on the environmental and social impact of carbon offset projects, leveraging verified data from IoT devices and on-site audits.
- ESG Reporting Dashboard: Provide a user-friendly dashboard for companies to view and manage their carbon credit holdings and associated ESG metrics.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Forest green (#3F704F), evoking nature and sustainability.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC), offering a clean and neutral backdrop.
- Accent color: Sky blue (#87CEEB), representing clarity and environmental awareness.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font providing a clean and modern look.
- Use minimalist icons representing environmental elements like trees, water, and renewable energy sources.
- Clean, intuitive layout with clear data visualizations and interactive maps.
- Subtle animations for data updates and user interactions to enhance engagement.
